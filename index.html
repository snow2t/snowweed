BOTANICAL INFORMATION

Learn the Basics

Introductory information about common terms and plant components.

Cannabinoids

Cannabinoids are chemical compounds found in cannabis. THC and CBD are two commonly discussed examples.

Terpenes

Terpenes are aromatic compounds found throughout nature. They contribute to the scents of many plants.

Plant Anatomy

Cannabis, like other plants, has roots, stems, leaves, flowers, and specialized surface structures.

Flower

The term flower refers to the plant's flowering structures, which contain several distinct botanical parts.

Extract Terminology

Rosin, resin, wax, shatter, and badder are terms used to describe categories or textures of extracts.

Product Formats

Flower, pre-rolls, and cartridges are different format terms used in discussions of cannabis products.
